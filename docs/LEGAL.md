## Do not rely on the examples for safety or money decisions

Examples such as `around`, `sky-watch` and `repo-radar` show data from free public services,
which can be late, wrong, rate limited or gone. Do not use them to decide anything about
safety, health, travel, weather warnings, earthquakes, spaceflight, money or security. Check
the official source. An example's sample data is invented.
